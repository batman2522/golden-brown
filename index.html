Poetry Collection #1

A poem describing your effortless beauty

OH SHE HAS NO IDEA HOW BEAUTIFUL SHE IS IN MY EYES. Not just in the way she smiles, or how her eyes seem to carry their own kind of light, but in the way she exists so effortlessly without knowing how much she means to me. You came when I had stopped looking for anyone, when I had convinced myself that love was something meant for other people. Then you arrived, and suddenly every wall I built began to feel unnecessary. From that moment, I already knew what was going to happen. I knew my heart would choose you without hesitation. I knew your name would become my favorite thought. I knew that somehow, among billions of people, you would become the one I wanted most. And maybe the strangest part is this: you probably have no idea. No idea that my days feel brighter because of you. No idea that I catch myself smiling when your name appears on my screen. No idea that every version of my future somehow finds a place for you in it. Oh, she has no idea how beautiful she is in my eyes.

Redefining Beauty

A poem proving no metaphor could ever match you

LOVE-CAM 2000

Photo 1: Pure Elegance
They asked me to describe you. So I searched for beautiful words. I borrowed lines from poets. I stole metaphors from the stars. I compared you to the moon, to roses, to spring. Then I erased every single one of them, because I realized they all had the same flaw: The moon has scars. Roses lose their petals. Spring always ends. But every time I look at you, I have to invent a new definition of beauty, because the old one was never enough.

A poem of endless devotion

10/10 Best Person
Teach me how to love you. Show me the parts of you that need patience, the places that need gentleness, the truths that need to be held and not questioned. Find peace in this knowing: I will love you as you deserve, In this life, and in every life that dares to follow.
